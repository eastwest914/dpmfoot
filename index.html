Frequently Asked Questions

Answers from Your Local Foot Doctor

What does DPM mean, and what does a podiatrist treat?

DPM stands for Doctor of Podiatric Medicine — a physician who specializes exclusively in the foot, ankle, and lower leg. As your local Dpm Foot Doctor, we diagnose and treat everything from foot pain and bunions to sports injuries, nail conditions, and diabetic foot complications.

When should I see a foot pain doctor?

If foot or ankle pain lasts more than a few days, worsens with activity, or is accompanied by swelling, numbness, or difficulty walking, it's time to see a Foot Doctor. Early treatment often prevents small issues from becoming bigger problems.

Do you treat diabetic foot conditions?

Yes. As your Ankle Doctor and foot care team, we offer regular diabetic foot screenings, wound care, and preventive management to help reduce the risk of infection, ulcers, and other complications associated with diabetes.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your first visit with our Foot Specialist includes a full discussion of your symptoms and health history, a hands-on foot and ankle exam, and in many cases, on-site diagnostics. We'll walk you through your diagnosis and treatment options before you leave.
